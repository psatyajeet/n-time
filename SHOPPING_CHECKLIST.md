# Parts for Transit Glow

| Part | Buy or bring |
| --- | --- |
| Adafruit Matrix Portal Starter Kit | [Kit #4812](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4812), listed at $69.95 before tax and shipping when checked. Includes the MatrixPortal M4, a 64×32 RGB panel, LED diffusion acrylic and adhesive, and a 5 V 2.4 A supply with USB-C adapter. |
| USB-C data cable | Bring one for flashing and copying files from your computer. |
| Stand or mount | [Bent-wire stand #1679](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1679) for a desk, or choose a mount once you have measured the panel, controller, and cable clearance. |
| Small screwdriver | For attaching the panel's power leads. |

If sourcing separately, match the [MatrixPortal M4 #4745](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4745)
with a compatible 64×32 RGB HUB75 panel and appropriately rated 5 V power
supply. The firmware uses the M4's AirLift network interface. Use the kit's
panel for the documented build. If size matters, a compatible 64×32 panel
with a smaller pitch changes the physical dimensions while keeping the
layout. Check scan compatibility, power needs, and diffuser dimensions before
buying a different panel.
